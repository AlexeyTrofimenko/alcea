
The Shadow Blade smirked, drawing his dagger slowly. "Did you really think hiding behind your fancy title would scare me, princess?"

"Careful, thief," Princess Althea warned coldly, her voice betraying none of the fear that gripped her chest. "The Knights of Silvermoon won’t be far behind."

The assassin laughed softly, stepping closer. He could feel her heartbeat quickening, taste the panic she tried to hide.

*No,* she thought desperately. *I mustn't show weakness.*
